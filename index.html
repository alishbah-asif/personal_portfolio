Hajj & Umrah AR Glasses Interface

A team project designing a hands-free AR glasses interface that helps pilgrims count tawaf rounds, navigate crowded areas, and meet up with family, built around no-touch interaction (side click and voice).

Design Process:

Three user interviews (8 findings), a user persona, 21 functional requirements, and a low-fidelity storyboard of 20+ screens and states that includes error and edge cases. Now building a high-fidelity Figma prototype for usability testing.
